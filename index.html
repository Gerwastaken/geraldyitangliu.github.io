About

Learning to sense.
Learning to interact.

I am a senior undergraduate student majoring in Automation at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research interests center on robot learning, robotic manipulation, and proprioceptive sensing for humanoid robots.

I have worked on whole-body contact localization and force estimation at Duke University, as well as learning manipulation policies from human demonstrations at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My experience spans simulation data collection, model training, evaluation, and deployment on a real robotic arm. I have also explored multi-agent systems based on large language models and machine learning for scientific computing.

Research experience

Duke UniversityJul - Oct 2026

Proprioceptive Force Sensing for Humanoid Robots

Research Intern · Advisor: Prof. Xianyi Cheng

  • Investigated whole-body contact localization and force estimation from proprioceptive signals, with a focus on transferring sensing models across humanoid control policies.
  • Contributed to a residual-conditioned force sensing framework combining a momentum observer, temporal learning, and privileged teacher-student training.
  • Designed and coordinated simulation data collection, baseline comparisons, and ablation studies, and audited data splits, calibration, and link-level evaluation.
  • Diagnosed motion-induced and self-contact false alarms through trajectory replay and contact telemetry, and evaluated targeted fine-tuning and cross-policy regression.
Shanghai Jiao Tong UniversityJul 2025 - May 2026

Learning Robotic Manipulation from Human Demonstrations

Research Intern · Advisor: Prof. Chuan Wen

  • Studied manipulation policies for unstructured home environments, focusing on data collection efficiency and task generalization.
  • Developed object keypoint detection policies and designed an Image-to-Flow-to-Act framework using human demonstration videos.
  • Independently built an end-to-end pipeline covering data acquisition, policy training, and execution on a real robotic arm.
